Chapter 02 · The builder

One person,a wide machine shop.

Blaxk is a developer and automation builder, and a mechatronics engineering student — which is why the work here keeps crossing from software into hardware-adjacent territory: computer vision, local speech models, network tooling, robotics-adjacent interfaces.

The through-line is not a framework. It is a preference: build the version that runs on your own machine, understand the whole stack, and publish the evidence.

Works in: React · Node & Express · Django REST · Python · TypeScript · Supabase · MongoDB · Playwright · Linux

Chapter 03 · Work · transition 01

Teaching machinesthat stay on your desk.

EduBlaxk turns a lecture PDF into diagnostic questions and grades theory against a rubric. SABI is the spoken half: you talk to it, it answers from your own slides, and the listening and speaking happen on your device — Whisper and on-device voices, never a cloud speech API.

Blaxk Xposer takes the opposite tack and moves your localhost outward instead: one QR code puts a dev server that is already running on your laptop in the hands of every phone on the Wi-Fi, rewriting headers and bodies so the dev server never notices it moved.
