About · the builder

Blaxk

I’m Blaxk. I build software, usually where it touches something physical or runs short on room: speech models on a laptop, vision on a webcam, a phone talking to your dev server. Things that keep working when the connection doesn’t.

I’m studying mechatronics engineering. I learned to build by shipping things people actually used, then going back to fix what broke at 2am.

I’d rather hand you the version that runs on your own machine than the one that needs a server I’m paying for. I like knowing what’s underneath the layer I’m working on. And I’d rather tell you a project is half-finished than have you find out after you’ve trusted me with something.
