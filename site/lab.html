The lab

Ideas I’ve worked on and haven’t finished. Some I’m still pulling toward. Some I parked on purpose. Each entry says what stage it actually reached and why it stopped there.

Nothing moves off this page until there’s a repo with working code in it. No prices, no promises, no “coming soon”.

The experience prototypes behind this site

This site was not designed by picking a template. Before any production code was written, three radically different navigation and scrolling concepts were built, run on desktop and mobile, measured for frame rate and long tasks, scored against six weighted criteria — and the loser of that contest is still here to read.

Concept A — Spatial Constellation

Projects as a navigable field with a camera that travels. The most original idea in the set, and the fastest after a measured compositing fix. Reused here as the work field.

Scored 81/100

Concept B — Source Reactor

An engineering instrument: projects as modules, source as a readable artifact on the same stage. Its navigation model won and now drives this site's rail, palette and source chamber.

Scored 85/100 · runner-up by one point

Concept C — Infinite Signal

A cinematic editorial journey. Selected as the spine of BlaxkVERSE because its story is carried by typography and real content — the most honest substrate for a portfolio about evidence.

Scored 86/100 · selected

Measurements, scores, screenshots of every state and the full rationale are recorded in docs/experience-decision.md and docs/performance-report.md in the project repository, alongside the runnable prototypes themselves.

What the lab is for

Experiments exist to answer questions that a specification cannot. Can speech recognition run locally fast enough to feel like conversation? Can a phone be handed a local dev server without a tunnel? Can a device with no internet still be a decent tutor? Each shipped project on this site started as one of those questions.

Bring a question to the lab

If you have a problem that needs a prototype before it needs a product — a feasibility check, a spike, a proof that a local-first approach can work — that is exactly the kind of brief this page exists for.
