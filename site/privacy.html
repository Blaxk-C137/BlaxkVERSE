Privacy & AI disclosure

What this site does with your data

The short version: almost nothing, because it asks for almost nothing. This page documents that precisely rather than reassuringly.

1 · Data this site collects

None that identifies you.

  • No analytics. No Google Analytics, Plausible, Umami, Fathom or similar. No counting script of any kind.
  • No cookies. None are set, which is why there is no cookie banner — a banner for cookies that do not exist would be theatre.
  • No third-party requests. No CDN, no webfonts, no embedded video, no social widgets, no tracking pixels. Every asset is served from this site.
  • No fingerprinting. Nothing reads your canvas signature, device list or fonts for identification.
  • No persistent storage of your activity. Motion preference is carried in the URL, not written to your device.

2 · The assistant panel

This is the part most sites are vague about, so here it is exactly.

  • No language model is connected in this build. The panel is a deterministic retriever over a fixed set of verified records that ship with the site. It matches your question against keywords and returns the matching record.
  • Nothing you type leaves your browser. There is no request to any API, ours or anyone else's. Open your browser's network tab and confirm it — that is the point of building it this way first.
  • No conversation is stored. The log lives in the page and disappears when you close or reload it.
  • It will not answer beyond its records. If your question is not covered, it says so and offers the contact route instead of improvising.
  • Its answers cite the record used, so you can check the claim against the page it came from.

The designed production behaviour

The intended production version replaces keyword matching with retrieval over approved content plus a Gemini call, with these rules fixed in advance:

  • The API key stays server-side and is never exposed to the browser through a public environment variable.
  • Input length, content type and output schema are validated; responses are structured and schema-checked before rendering.
  • Per-session and per-IP rate limits, token ceilings and spend ceilings, with abuse controls and observability.
  • Repository text, READMEs and issue comments are treated as untrusted input. Prompt injection is assumed, not hoped against, and the assistant has no tools and no write access.
  • No persistent chat history by default. If retention is ever enabled, it will be disclosed here before it is switched on.
  • Popular answers cached; a deterministic static answer always available when the model fails or the budget is exhausted.
  • Every AI answer is labelled and links to the source it used. The assistant never claims to have read a whole repository when only excerpts were provided.

3 · The contact form

This build has no server, so the form validates your input in the browser and then tells you plainly that nothing was transmitted. It does not silently drop your message and it does not pretend to have sent it.

In production the form posts to a server-side handler that validates and rate-limits the input, sends the message, and stores nothing beyond what is needed to reply. Retention would be stated here before that endpoint is switched on.

4 · Motion, accessibility and your preferences

  • Your system's reduced-motion preference is honoured automatically, and can be overridden with the motion control in the navigation or the M key.
  • The chosen motion level is stored in the URL (?motion=off) so a reduced-motion link can be shared — and so nothing is written to your device.
  • Content is identical in every motion mode. The reduced route removes movement, never information.

5 · Code shown on this site

  • Excerpts come from public repositories and are pinned to a commit SHA, path and line range.
  • They are screened for credentials, secrets, personal data and internal endpoints before publication.
  • They are rendered as inert, selectable text. Nothing from a repository is ever executed in your browser.
  • Third-party forks are credited to their authors and excluded from the portfolio rather than exhibited.

6 · Contact for privacy questions

Use the contact page. If you believe something on this site misrepresents a fact, a project or a person, say so — corrections to the record take priority over presentation.
